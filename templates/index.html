✳ ABOUT LEAFCARE AI

Technology with a greener purpose.

LeafCare AI is a plant leaf image classification project that combines computer vision and deep learning to help identify potential plant diseases.

Its classification system uses transfer learning with MobileNetV2, trained on a multi-plant dataset containing 38 image classes.
